## What is Heavenly Pheonix?
Heavenly Pheonix is the revival of **Heavenly Ascended**, a – mainly EU –  TSB clan that merge into another clan: Kaizen.
With this, **Heavenly Ascended** has left the realm of individuality and is now existing as part of Kaizen.
> [!NOTE]
> DO NOT blame or harass the leaders of either **Heavenly Ascended** and **Kaizen** as they had their reasons for the merge.
> Any actions made against them will lead to punishments in the clan and can also come with a forceful ban/blacklist!

## Why Heavenly Pheonix?
The name is to resemble Heavenly Ascended with a bit of change: Pheonix – a fictional being that is said to be capable of reviving itself and such is the plan with the current clan.

## What's different?
Heavenly Ascended was a nice clan, and frankly a good community to be part of. But with time, every good thing has to come to an end; and as fate would have it, Heavenly Ascended was on its death bed: barely anyone chatting, tryouts slowed, events stopped.

What we *can* offer is still a mystery, but what we __aim__ to offer is something we are able to name:
- **Organization**: Heavenly Ascended was quite organized on its own, but we plan to do it on a higher scale.
- **Individuality**: Our clan will go on to stay its own entity – no merges, only allies.
- **Dependency**: Heavenly Ascended had a bot, but it was quite simple and was not much to use. The new revolution brings a new style; our bot will be our greatest asset and have no issues with handling multiple actions required. In fact, it is planned to manage the whole clan without a slither of an issue – and with that, we are to add bots only for functions we are incapable of using natively without running into issues.

Alongside that, we are able to share that all our services will have been assigned a webpage, such you are able to see in this repository.

## Will you succeed?
We cannot say for sure, but we hope to bring our once beloved clan back from the ashes it has fallen into.
