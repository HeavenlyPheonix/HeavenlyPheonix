<h1 align="center">Heavenly Pheonix</h1>
<p align="center">The revival of what once was</p>

## What is Heavenly Pheonix?
Heavenly Pheonix is the revival of **Heavenly Ascended**, a – mainly EU –  TSB clan that merge into another clan: Kaizen.
With this, **Heavenly Ascended** has left the realm of individuality and is now existing as part of Kaizen.
> [!NOTE]
> DO NOT blame or harass the leaders of either **Heavenly Ascended** and **Kaizen** as they had their reasons for the merge.
> Any actions made against them will lead to punishments in the clan and can also come with a forceful ban/blacklist!

## Why Heavenly Pheonix?
The name is to resemble Heavenly Ascended with a bit of change: Pheonix – a fictional being that is said to be capable of reviving itself and such is the plan with the current clan.
